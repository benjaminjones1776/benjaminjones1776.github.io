This is a tool aims to provide a quick and easy water analysis to cities, real-estate developers and firefighters. It calculates the static head water pressure around a proposed water tank.

Note: This is a 3D Map. Press (ctrl/command) key & drag the mouse to enter 3D mode.

🔥 FIRE OPERATIONS

Firefighters can now see how their temporary water tanks can best supply water pressure where and when they need it.
Pressure grid: 6 square miles Resolution: Medium, 15.84 ft

🏠 URBAN PLANNER

This tool saves time and money when a new water tank is needed. Pressure grid: 1 square mile Resolution: High, 2.64 ft
