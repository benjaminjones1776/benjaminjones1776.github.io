Hydrostatic WUI Risk Modeler

This is a tool created to bridge the gap between the Municipalities and other groups like Real Estate Developers and FireFighters. The strongest feature of this tool is that it calculates a water pressure grid of the surrounding area around a picked point.
When using this tool you may access the 3D Mapping by using your (ctrl/command) key & dragging the mouse.

🔥 FIRE OPERATIONS

This button focuses on letting firefighters place a temporary water tank (usually called a pumpkin) then see how the resulting water pressure can help fight the fire.

🏠 URBAN PLANNER

This button focuses on helping city water engineers and developers come to an understanding for needed water tanks for new subdivisions.
